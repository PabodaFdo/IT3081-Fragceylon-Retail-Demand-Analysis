GENERAL INTERPRETATION LIMITATIONS
- Historical observational data do not establish causality.
- Japan transactions are structurally concentrated within one buyer and the Main Dealer/current-regime structure.
- Repeated transactions from the same buyers may violate complete observation-level independence.
- The models predict transaction-level Quantity, not aggregate monthly demand.
- The Apr-Jun 2026 holdout period is one future evaluation period and does not guarantee identical future performance.
- Regularized-model coefficients are affected by shrinkage and should not be interpreted like ordinary unpenalized regression coefficients.
