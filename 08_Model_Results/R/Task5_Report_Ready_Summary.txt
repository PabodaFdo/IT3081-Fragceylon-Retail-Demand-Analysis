TASK 5 - PREDICTIVE STATISTICAL MODELLING

Prediction objective:
Estimate future Regular Order Quantity under Fragceylon's current post-April-2025 business structure.

MODELLING POPULATION
Current-regime rows: 2014 
Current-regime dates: 2025-04-01 to 2026-06-30
Response: Quantity

TRAIN-TEST STRATEGY
Training: 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31; n = 1599 
Testing : 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-30; n = 415 
A chronological split was used because the practical objective is prediction of future transactions.

PREDICTOR DECISIONS
Initial candidates: Product Name, Export Market, Month, Day of Week and Time Trend.
Channel Type and Sales Regime were excluded because both are constant in the current-regime modelling population.
Buyer was excluded from the primary model to reduce customer-specific memorization.
Total Value was excluded because it is directly derived from Quantity and would cause target leakage.

BASELINE
Training mean prediction: 306.5503 
Test MAE: 137.0608 
Test RMSE: 178.3421 
Test R-squared: -0.005 

INITIAL FULL MLR
Formula: Quantity ~ Product Name + Export Market + Month + Day of Week + Time Trend
Training R-squared: 0.326319 
Adjusted R-squared: 0.316915 
Test MAE: 118.1498 
Test RMSE: 140.1878 
Test R-squared: 0.379 
Diagnostics identified severe multicollinearity between Month indicators and Time Trend and statistically significant heteroscedasticity.

TRAINING-ONLY SPECIFICATION COMPARISON
The reduced specification using Product Name, Export Market and Day of Week achieved the strongest balance of average chronological validation error, stability, parsimony and interpretability.

FINAL SELECTED MLR
Formula: Quantity ~ Product Name + Export Market + Day of Week
Training R-squared: 0.31838 
Adjusted R-squared: 0.314088 
Test MAE: 116.8403 
Test RMSE: 137.9482 
Test R-squared: 0.3987 
Test MAE improvement over baseline: 14.75 %
Test RMSE improvement over baseline: 22.65 %

ROBUST INFERENCE
HC3 heteroscedasticity-robust standard errors were used for coefficient uncertainty because the Breusch-Pagan diagnostic indicated unequal residual variance.
The Japan coefficient is large and positive in the selected model, but it must be interpreted as an association rather than a causal market effect because Japan transactions are structurally concentrated within one buyer and the Main Dealer/post-April regime.

LIMITATIONS
- Historical observational data do not establish causality.
- Repeated transactions from the same buyers may violate complete observation-level independence.
- Japan transactions are concentrated within one buyer structure.
- The model predicts transaction-level Quantity, not aggregate monthly demand.
- Test results describe the Apr-Jun 2026 holdout period and should not be treated as guaranteed future accuracy.
- The 41 valid high-volume orders were retained; no observations were deleted simply because they were influential.
