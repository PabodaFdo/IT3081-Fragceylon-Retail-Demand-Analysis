TASK 9 - CRITICAL EVALUATION OF TIME SERIES ANALYSIS

CURRENT DATA HISTORY
The full Regular Order history runs from 2024-10-18 to 2026-06-30, approximately 1.7 years. However, a major structural channel change occurred on 2025-04-01. The current stable Main Dealer regime therefore contains only 15 monthly observations from 2025-04-01 to 2026-06-01.

TREND
A simple current-regime monthly linear trend was estimated only as a descriptive diagnostic. It should not be treated as a production forecasting model because the series is short.

Monthly trend coefficient: 1244.943
Trend p-value: 0.6503

SEASONALITY
The current stable regime covers only about 1.25 years. This provides too few repeated annual cycles to support dependable conclusions about yearly seasonality. There are only 3 direct same-month comparisons separated by 12 months in the current regime.

ARIMA / SARIMA
ARIMA and SARIMA are conceptually relevant for future demand forecasting, but a dependable model should not be forced onto the current short, structurally changing history. SARIMA is especially difficult to justify because annual seasonal parameters require repeated comparable seasonal cycles.

BUSINESS APPLICATION
With a longer stable history, time-series forecasting could support product-level demand planning, seasonal preparation, purchasing schedules, dealer planning and short-horizon inventory decisions. Forecasts should be generated separately by perfume where sufficient data exist.

DATA COLLECTION RECOMMENDATION
Fragceylon should continue collecting consistent product-level sales data and add stock availability, stock-out indicators, promotion data, price history and inventory information. Future forecast validation should use rolling-origin or future holdout evaluation.
